# AI Risk Management & Governance Program

Comprehensive AI risk governance framework aligned to NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF) for enterprises deploying AI systems at scale.

---

## 4 Phases

**Phase 1: Foundation & Governance**  
3-layer governance structure, 7 AI system types, 4-dimensional risk scoring (Threat × Vulnerability × Impact × Trust), NIST AI RMF alignment (GOVERN/MAP/MEASURE/MANAGE), compliance framework mapping (NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act, sector-specific)

**Phase 2: Inventory & Assessment**  
AI system discovery (7 sources), master inventory, criticality assessment, risk scoring applied to 24+ systems, portfolio analysis, assessment prioritization roadmap

**Phase 3: Assessment & Risk Mitigation**  
Risk-based assessment procedures (CRITICAL: bias testing, adversarial robustness, performance, explainability, data audit, human oversight), 5 mitigation control areas, remediation tracking, assessment reporting framework

**Phase 4: Monitoring, Compliance & Governance**  
Continuous monitoring (real-time to daily by criticality), automated compliance tracking (NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act, sector-specific), re-assessment schedule (annual to as-needed), escalation procedures, executive dashboards

## CISO Questions This Program Answers

**What issues are we bringing into the company?**
→ Phase 3 (Assessment & Risk Mitigation) identifies and fixes AI-specific risks (bias, adversarial, drift, etc.)

**Are we staying in compliance?**
→ Phase 4 (Continuous Compliance Tracking) automatically monitors NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act, and sector-specific requirements in real-time

**When to assess again?**
→ Phase 4 (Re-Assessment Schedule) defines assessment timing (annual for CRITICAL, biennial for HIGH, triennial for MEDIUM, as-needed for LOW)

**How do we ensure AI doesn't come back to bite us?**
→ Phase 4 (Continuous Monitoring & Governance) provides ongoing oversight, escalation procedures, and governance framework ensuring long-term risk management
